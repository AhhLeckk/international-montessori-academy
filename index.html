✨ Our Mission

A learning environment built on Love, Harmony & Beauty

Our mission is to provide an atmosphere of learning that is LOVING emotionally, and physically and spiritually SAFE — one that creates a living and unique Montessori environment where children learn through "doing."

We instill tolerance, thoughtfulness, consideration, and responsibility. We celebrate differences in others. The focus is on the heart of the child, while studying their strengths and passions. International Montessori Academy values diversity and seeks membership families of all racial, cultural, religious, and economic backgrounds.

Founded in 1964 by Inayat Unissa Inayat-Khan Bergum — whose grandfather Hazrat Inayat Khan personally met with Dr. Maria Montessori — IMA carries a legacy of uniting children through Love, Harmony, and Beauty.

❤️
Love
Children are born with the natural tendency to love. Love is the platform from which children learn most effectively.
🕊️
Harmony
When there is understanding and knowledge, harmony is reached. We rise above differences together.
🌸
Beauty
We present moral teachings without doctrines and inspire children to have ideals and find beauty in learning.
🧠
Follow the Child
We create independent learners who attain their personal best at their own level, speed, and rhythm of development.
📚 Our Programs

Programs for every stage of childhood

From infants as young as 3 months through elementary K–8, each program is rooted in the Montessori method — where children are free to discover, explore, and create in a prepared, enriched environment.

👶 Infant Program
Infant Care
Ages 3 months – 24 months
A gentle, nurturing environment for our youngest learners. Our trained caregivers provide individualized attention with a remarkable 1:4 ratio, supporting early development through sensory exploration and loving care.
🌱 Toddler Program
Toddler Montessori
Ages 2 – 3 years
Toddlers thrive in a prepared environment designed for their growing independence. With a 1:6 ratio, children develop language, motor skills, and early social foundations through purposeful Montessori activity.
🎨 Primary / Preschool
Primary Montessori
Ages 2½ – 5 years
The heart of the Montessori experience for young children. Students work with the classic Montessori materials, develop independence, and build the foundational skills for a lifetime of learning in a 1:10 environment.
🔬 Elementary
Elementary K–8
Kindergarten through 8th Grade (varies by campus)
Elementary students engage in the Cosmic Curriculum — an integrated study of language arts, mathematics, science, history, and the arts. Research, collaboration, and independent inquiry are central to every school day.
🧩 Montessori Materials
Self-Directed Learning
All age groups
Montessori materials are self-directed and self-correcting — activating in children a desire to learn more. Each material develops one perception at a time and places the responsibility for finding answers on the child.

❓ Common Questions

Things families ask us

What are your student-to-teacher ratios?+
Our ratios are among the best in early childhood education: 1:4 for Infants, 1:6 for Toddlers, and 1:10 for Preschool and Elementary. This ensures every child receives genuinely individualized attention throughout their day.
What is the Montessori method?+
Developed in the 1900s by Dr. Maria Montessori — a scientist, mathematician, and physician — the Montessori method is based on the premise that each child is born with a drive to develop themselves. A child learns best through physical activity and the senses, given freedom to discover, explore, and create in an enriched environment with supportive encouragement.
What are Montessori materials and why are they different?+
Montessori materials are self-directed and self-correcting — they activate a desire to learn more because they engage children both physically and mentally. Each material develops one perception at a time and places the responsibility for finding answers on the child, building independence and self-motivation rather than dependency on the teacher.
Who are your teachers?+
All lead teachers hold Montessori credentials and are highly qualified, experienced, innovative, and creative. Our teachers emphasize moral instruction, tolerance, and kindness. They are supported by ECE-qualified teacher's assistants at every campus.
Do you accept families of different backgrounds and faiths?+
Absolutely. International Montessori Academy values diversity and actively seeks membership families of all racial, cultural, religious, and economic backgrounds. Our founding philosophy of Love, Harmony, and Beauty was built on celebrating differences — not minimizing them.
What extracurricular activities are available?+
Extracurricular activities vary by campus and by season. Please visit your specific campus page on our website, or contact the campus director, to learn which activities and enrichment programs are currently being offered.
How do I find tuition and fees?+
Tuition varies by campus and program level. Please visit our Tuition & Fees page or contact your preferred campus directly. We encourage all interested families to reach out regardless of financial situation — we are committed to making Montessori education accessible.
